VICEVERSE

About

Last updated 15 Nov 2026

VICEVERSE is an independent, fan-made hub for the world of Leonida. We built it to make three things easy: exploring the regions, browsing vehicles and characters, and telling confirmed news apart from rumours.

Our editorial standards

  • Every news item carries a label: Confirmed, Reported or Rumour.
  • We link to primary or credible sources and name them.
  • We write in our own words and do not copy official text or artwork.
  • We do not publish leaks or present guesses as facts.
  • We correct mistakes and show an update date.

How we are funded

VICEVERSE is free to use. We may show advertising, such as Google AdSense, to cover running costs. Ads never change how we label or rank information.

Not affiliated

VICEVERSE is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. All trademarks belong to their owners.

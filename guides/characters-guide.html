GTA VI characters: who is who in Leonida

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · Independent fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games

This is a spoiler-free guide to the characters Rockstar has introduced on its official site, written in our own words. Browse them any time in the Characters section, where you can also save your favourites.

The protagonists

Lucia Caminos

A fighter trained by her father. She has spent time in Leonida Penitentiary and wants to build the life her mother always dreamed of for her.

Jason Duval

Raised in the Keys among criminals, Jason served in the Army and then worked for local drug runners. He may be ready for a change.

In the Keys

Cal Hampton

Jason's friend, happiest at home listening in on Coast Guard communications.

Brian Heder

An older drug runner from the Keys' smuggling era. He lets Jason live rent-free in exchange for help with local shakedowns.

In Vice City

Boobie Ike

A businessman with interests in real estate, a strip club and a recording studio, and a stake in a label partnership with Dre'Quan.

Dre'Quan Priest

A hustler who always wanted a music career and has signed the Real Dimez to his label.

Bae-Luxe and Roxy (Real Dimez)

High-school friends who turned shaking down dealers into a rap career. They have a strong social media presence and are signed to Only Raw Records.

The crew

Raul Bautista

A bank robber who takes big risks for big rewards. His crew will have to escalate or back out.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the main characters?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval are the two protagonists.

Are these descriptions official?

They are based on Rockstar's official character profiles, summarised in our own words.

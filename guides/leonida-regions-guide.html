Leonida regions explained: all six areas of GTA VI

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · Independent fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games

The world of GTA VI is the fictional US state of Leonida. Rockstar's official site names six regions. This guide summarises what is known about each one, in our own words, and says clearly where details are not confirmed. You can also explore them on the interactive map.

Vice City

The series' return to a classic setting: a neon-lit coastal metropolis built around nightlife, excess and heavy traffic. Neighbourhoods mentioned in official material include Ocean Beach and Little Cuba, and the city has a major cruise port.

Leonida Keys

A tropical island chain with boat parties, dive bars and underwater areas for scuba diving. It is the home region of Jason Duval, one of the two protagonists.

Grassrivers

Leonida's wetlands. Expect stilt-house communities, alligators and other dangerous wildlife, with alligator hunting among the activities shown.

Port Gellhorn

A once-popular tourist destination that has fallen into decline. It has abandoned attractions and a growing drug trade.

Ambrosia

An industrial region centred on a sugar refinery, where a local biker gang enforces its own rules.

Mount Kalaga National Park

A large wilderness along the northern border of the state, with hunting, fishing and off-road trails, plus isolated residents in the backwoods.

What is not confirmed

Region names and descriptions are official. The exact positions, sizes and borders are not. The VICEVERSE map is a schematic that places the regions by rough relative position, so use it for orientation, not navigation. More small areas are expected beyond these six.

Frequently asked questions

How many regions are there?

Rockstar names six: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

Is the VICEVERSE map official?

No. It is an unofficial schematic made by fans. Positions are approximate.

Is Vice City the only city?

It is the main metropolis named, but other towns and areas exist, such as Port Gellhorn.

Sources: Rockstar Games (official site), GamesRadar.

GTA VI release date, editions and pre-order bonuses

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · Independent fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, 19 November 2026. This page collects what Rockstar and the platform holders have confirmed, and separates it from what outlets only report. We review it regularly and mark the date at the top.

  • Release date: 19 November 2026, announced by Rockstar after an earlier move from 26 May 2026.
  • Xbox cloud play: streaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1am ET on 19 November (Xbox Wire, 10 Nov 2026).
  • Pre-install: Xbox Series X|S owners can pre-install the game now. Xbox One can only stream it.
  • Listed prices (Microsoft Store): Standard Edition $79.99, Ultimate Edition $99.99.
  • Pre-order bonus: orders placed before 23:59:59 on 19 November include the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+.
Prices, bonuses and availability can differ by store and region. Always confirm on your own store page before you buy.

How you can play

The game was announced for the current console generation. On Xbox, Series X|S owners can install it natively, while Xbox One players can stream it through the cloud. According to Xbox, cloud streaming uses Xbox Series S graphics settings, and players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime without a Game Pass subscription starting 11 November.

A PC version has not been announced in the sources we reviewed. Fans often expect one eventually, but that is speculation, so treat any PC date you see online as a guess.

Editions and the pre-order pack

The Microsoft Store lists two editions: Standard at $79.99 and Ultimate at $99.99. The Vintage Vice City Pack and a month of GTA+ come with pre-orders made before the end of launch day. We do not list what is inside Ultimate beyond the price, because we have not confirmed a full contents list from a first-party source.

When does it unlock?

Several outlets report that the game unlocks at midnight local time in each region, which would let earlier time zones start first. Outlets differ on details and it could change, so this is labelled reported, not confirmed. The Xbox cloud start time of 1am ET on 19 November is confirmed by Xbox.

Has the game gone gold?

One outlet reported on 15 November that development is complete and the launch is on track. We have not seen a Rockstar statement, and games can still slip after reaching gold, so treat it as a report.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GTA VI release date?

19 November 2026, per Rockstar's announcement.

Can I play on Xbox One?

Only by streaming through Xbox Cloud Gaming, according to Xbox Wire. Native installs are for Series X|S.

How much does it cost?

The Microsoft Store lists $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate. Other stores and regions can differ.

Is there a PC version?

None has been announced in the sources we reviewed.
